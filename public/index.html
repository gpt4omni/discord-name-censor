How it works
  1. Add one or more screenshots: drop, paste with Ctrl+V, or pick files.
  2. Drag a box around your name. Every identical copy, in every screenshot, gets covered.
  3. Do the same for your avatar, and for the small name and avatar in reply lines.
  4. Click a box to switch a single match off or on. Lower a slider if some copies were missed.
  5. Hold Space to peek at the original. Ctrl+Z undoes. ← → switch screenshots.

Matching needs the same Discord zoom level. A screenshot taken at a different zoom needs its own boxes.

Nothing is uploaded. All processing stays in your browser.
