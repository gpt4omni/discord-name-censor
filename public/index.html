how to use
  1. add screenshots: drop them in, paste with ctrl+v, or pick files.
  2. drag a box around your name. every copy in every screenshot gets covered.
  3. do the same for your avatar, and the small name and avatar on reply lines.
  4. click a box to turn one match off or on. lower a slider if some copies got missed.
  5. hold space to see the original. ctrl+z undoes. ← → switch screenshots.

matching needs the same discord zoom level. a screenshot at a different zoom needs its own boxes.
